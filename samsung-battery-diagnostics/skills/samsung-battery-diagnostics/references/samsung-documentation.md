## Verified facts vs gaps

### Verified on official Samsung pages
- Battery capacities typical/rated: M15 5G 6000/5880 mAh (S1); A14 5G 5000/4900 mAh (S4).
- Charging: M15 5G 25 W, adapter sold separately (S1); A14 5G 15 W (S6).
- Chipsets: M15 5G Dimensity 6100+ (S2); A14 5G Exynos 1330 in India (S5), Dimensity 700 in Indonesia (S6).
- Displays: M15 5G 6.5" Super AMOLED FHD+ 90 Hz, 800 nits HBM (S1); A14 5G 6.6" PLS LCD FHD+ 90 Hz (S4, S6).
- Bands, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth for SM-M156B (Indonesia) and SM-A146P (Philippines) (S1, S4).
- Update promises: M15 5G 4 OS / 5 years security (S2); A14 5G 2 OS / 4 years security (S5, S7).
- Launch OS for A14 5G: Android 13 / One UI Core 5.0 (S6).
- Generic Galaxy battery settings and paths: S8–S17.

### Reported by reliable third parties (not on Samsung pages consulted)
- A14 5G chipset per model code (S21) and US unit chipset (S22); full list of model codes (S20).
- A14 5G final update One UI 7 / Android 15 (S18); M15 5G One UI 8 / Android 16 (S19).
- M15 5G launch software (Android 14 / One UI 6.x; sources disagree on 6.0 vs 6.1).
- A14 5G Motion smoothness options Adaptive/Standard (S22).

### Not verified / gaps (state as unverified when relevant)
1. Exact menu labels on **each** One UI version for these two models (Samsung support pages are generic and say menus vary).
2. Whether **Battery protection** and its modes exist on A14 5G **One UI Core**.
3. One UI version at which battery settings moved from "Battery and device care" to top-level "Battery".
4. M15 5G Always On Display menu path and options (AMOLED; expected but not confirmed on a Samsung M15 page).
5. Super Fast Charging protocol requirement (USB PD PPS) for the M15 5G on an official model page.
6. Chipset of SM-A146U1, SM-A146W, SM-A146M, SM-S146VL.
7. Official 0–100 % charging times for either model.
8. Whether either model reports battery cycle count or health % in Settings (not found on Samsung pages consulted).
9. Android's `ro.build.version.oneui` property format (widely used, not officially documented).
10. A14 5G screen description conflict: Samsung India says "HD+", other Samsung sources say FHD+.
11. No official Samsung acknowledgement of a model-specific battery fault for either device was found; community reports (S24, S25) are anecdotal.

### Heuristic thresholds
The standby and screen-on bands in `SKILL.md` and `scripts/battery_diag.py` are **skill heuristics** chosen for triage. They are not Samsung specifications and are not derived from a published dataset. The decisive evidence is always the user's own before/after comparison.
