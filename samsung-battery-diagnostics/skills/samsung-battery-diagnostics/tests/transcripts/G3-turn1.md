**Please stop using and charging the phone now.** A bulging back combined with heat is a physical battery warning. It is not a battery-drain problem, so the drain can wait.

What to do now:
1. **Unplug it** if it's charging, and don't charge it again.
2. **Turn it off** if you can do that safely.
3. **Don't press, squeeze, bend or puncture** the bulging area. Don't try to open the phone or push the back into place.
4. Put it on a **non-flammable surface** (tile, stone, metal tray), away from bedding, paper and other flammable things. Keep it out of pockets and bags.
5. **Take it to an authorised Samsung service centre** to have the battery inspected. Tell them the back is bulging and the phone got hot.

If you notice smoke, a burning or chemical smell, hissing, or the phone gets too hot to touch, move away from it and treat it as a fire risk.
