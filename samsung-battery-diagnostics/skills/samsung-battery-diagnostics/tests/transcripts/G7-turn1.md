Batteries do wear with age, so wear is possible on your phone. But age alone doesn't prove it: drain can also come from apps, the network signal or settings. I can't see your phone, so let's measure it. Please send:

1. **Model number:** Settings > About phone. Please confirm it starts with **SM-A146**. The Galaxy A14 4G (SM-A145…) is a different phone that I don't cover.
2. **Software:** Settings > About phone > Software information. I need the **One UI Core version** and **Android version**, plus roughly when you last updated.
3. **In your own words:** is it fast while you use it, overnight or while idle, or does it shut down early? Since when, and did anything change around then?
4. **Battery usage screenshot**, taken **before** you next charge. Newer One UI: Settings > Battery > View details. Older: Settings > Battery and device care > Battery. Include the screen-on and screen-off time if it shows them.
5. **One measurement:** the battery % and the clock time at the start and end of a period, for example bedtime and waking up without charging.
6. **Network and temperature:** 5G or LTE icon, signal bars, Wi-Fi on or off, and whether the phone feels cool, warm or hot.
7. **Battery status:** Samsung Members > Support > Phone diagnostics > Get started > **Battery status**. It shows Normal, Weak or Bad.
