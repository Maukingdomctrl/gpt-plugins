**I wouldn't factory reset it yet.** It erases everything and takes hours to set up again. It also often doesn't fix battery drain: if the cause is an app, restoring your apps can bring the problem straight back. And if the battery itself is worn, a reset can't fix that.

I'd only suggest a reset as a last resort, when all of these are true:
- Two rounds of targeted changes didn't help, measured under similar conditions.
- The drain still happens in **Safe mode**, where downloaded apps are switched off.
- Your software is up to date.
- Samsung Members says the battery is **Normal**. If it says Weak or Bad, the battery comes first.
- You have a full backup.

Even then, a software check at a Samsung service centre is worth trying first.

I can't see your phone, so let's find out what "bad" means on yours. Please send:

1. **Software:** Settings > About phone > Software information. I need the **One UI Core version** and the **Android version**, plus roughly when you last installed an update.
2. **In your own words:** does it drain fast while you use it, overnight or while idle, or does it shut down early? Since when, and did anything change around then (a new app, an update)?
3. **Battery status:** Samsung Members > Support > Phone diagnostics > Get started > **Battery status**. It shows Normal, Weak or Bad.
4. **Battery usage screenshot**, taken **before** you next charge. Newer One UI: Settings > Battery > View details. Older: Settings > Battery and device care > Battery. Include the screen-on and screen-off time if it shows them.
5. **One measurement:** the battery % and the clock time at the start and end of a period, for example bedtime and waking up without charging, or a few hours of normal use.
6. **Network and temperature:** 5G or LTE icon, signal bars, Wi-Fi on or off, and whether the phone feels cool, warm or hot.
7. **Shutdowns:** has it ever switched off while still showing charge? If so, at what %?
