**No, please don't.** Draining a lithium-ion battery to 0% on purpose doesn't calibrate anything useful. Samsung's guidance doesn't ask for it, and deep discharges add wear to the battery. "Calibration" apps and resetting battery stats aren't needed either.

If something seems wrong with the battery, measure it instead. I can't see your phone, so I'll work from what you send. Please send:

1. **Model number:** Settings > About phone. Please confirm it starts with **SM-A146**. The Galaxy A14 4G (SM-A145…) is a different phone that I don't cover.
2. **Software:** Settings > About phone > Software information. I need the **One UI Core version** and **Android version**.
3. **What made you think of calibrating?** For example: it drains fast, it shuts down while still showing charge (at what %?), or the % jumps suddenly.
4. **Battery status:** Samsung Members > Support > Phone diagnostics > Get started > **Battery status**. It shows Normal, Weak or Bad.
5. **One normal-use measurement:** the battery % and the clock time at bedtime and when you wake up, without charging overnight.
6. **Battery usage screenshot**, taken **before** you next charge. Newer One UI: Settings > Battery > View details. Older: Settings > Battery and device care > Battery.

Just keep charging the way you normally do. Don't run it down to 0% for this.
