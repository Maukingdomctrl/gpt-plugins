Good question, and the honest answer is: **it can, but it depends on your coverage**, so it's worth measuring on your phone rather than assuming.

**What Samsung says (official):** 5G can use more power because the phone may use 4G and 5G at the same time, which can also cause slight warming; Samsung suggests switching to LTE in areas with weak 5G. Where 5G signal is strong and stable, the difference may be small. So for your phone this is a **hypothesis to test**, not a conclusion.
