**I'd skip third-party battery saver, cleaner and RAM booster apps.** They can't fix the cause of the drain. Many of them run in the background themselves, and closing apps by force can make drain worse because those apps restart. Your phone already has Samsung's own tools:

- **Power saving:** newer One UI: Settings > Battery > Power saving.
  - *What it does:* limits background data and can lower performance and refresh rate. Options vary by version.
  - *Trade-off:* notifications and sync can be delayed, the phone runs slower, and it only hides the cause.
  - *To undo:* turn it off.
- **Background usage limits:** newer: Settings > Battery > Background usage limits. Older: Settings > Battery and device care > Battery > Background usage limits.
  - *What it does:* puts specific apps to sleep so they don't run in the background.
  - *Trade-off:* those apps may stop sending notifications and syncing. Don't use it on messaging, alarm, banking-OTP or work apps.
  - *To undo:* remove the app from the list.

Menu names change between One UI versions. If you can't find one, use the search icon in Settings.
