# Quality checklist (all must pass before "finished")

## Correctness
- [ ] Every numeric answer verified by calculation (`qa_report.txt`: 0 errors).
- [ ] Every "REVIEW" item (text, science, multiple-choice) re-read and confirmed, or flagged to the user.
- [ ] Answer key numbering matches the worksheet exactly.
- [ ] Word problems are solvable with the grade's skills and have one clear answer.
- [ ] Units and labels are correct (cm vs in, °F for US buyers unless metric is the point).

## Age and originality
- [ ] Vocabulary, numbers and amount of reading suit the stated grade.
- [ ] Content is original: no copied textbook or seller pages, no trademarked characters, no fonts or clip art without a licence that allows commercial use.
- [ ] Sensitive topics avoided; names and contexts are neutral and inclusive.

## Layout and print
- [ ] US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) for US buyers; A4 (8.27 × 11.69 in) version if offered.
- [ ] Margins ≥ 0.5 in; no section spills onto an extra page (no WARN line).
- [ ] Same title style, fonts, numbering and footer on every page.
- [ ] Name/Date line on student pages; clear one-sentence instructions per section.
- [ ] Enough writing space for the age group; prints legibly in black and white.

## Package
- [ ] Separate answer-key file.
- [ ] Cover shows title, grade, page count, paper sizes, and matches the contents.
- [ ] Previews are watermarked and show real pages, without giving the whole product away.
- [ ] File names are readable (e.g. `grade-3-multiplication_worksheets_letter.pdf`).
- [ ] Terms of use line (personal/single-classroom) present.
