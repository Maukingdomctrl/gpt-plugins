# India tax and compliance notes for selling digital downloads abroad

**Last checked: 2026-10-30.** This is general information, not tax or legal advice. Tell the user to confirm their own position with a chartered accountant or GST practitioner before registering, filing, or relying on an exemption.

## GST

| Point | Finding | Status / source |
|---|---|---|
| Normal threshold for service suppliers | Registration required when all-India aggregate turnover exceeds **₹20 lakh** (₹10 lakh in special category states). Aggregate turnover includes exports. | Verified – [CBIC GST registration FAQ (PDF)](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/faq-manual/faqs-registration.pdf); [CGST Act s.2(6)](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter1/section2_v1.00.html) |
| Inter-state supply of services | Exempt from compulsory registration up to the same turnover limits. | Verified – [Notification 10/2017-Integrated Tax](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/pdf/integrated-tax/10_2017_IT.pdf) |
| Selling downloads to foreign buyers | Likely treated as export of services (zero-rated). Whether a particular sale also counts as OIDAR, and whether any compulsory-registration rule applies, was **not settled** from official sources. | Unverified – ask a GST practitioner |
| Marketplace rules can be stricter than the law | Etsy requires a valid GSTIN for every Indian shop, even below ₹20 lakh. So on Etsy, GST registration is effectively mandatory. | Verified – [Etsy Help India](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/6742925359255) |
| Voluntary registration | Allowed; once registered you are a normal taxable person with return-filing duties (monthly/quarterly returns, even nil returns). That is an ongoing time cost. | Verified (registration FAQ above); filing frequency details: re-check |
| Exporting without paying IGST | Registered exporters file a **Letter of Undertaking (LUT, form GST RFD-11)** on the GST portal before zero-rated supplies; valid for the financial year. | Verified – [GST portal LUT guide](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/refund/Furnishing_of_Letter_of_Undertaking_for_Export_of_Goods_or_Services.htm) |
| Cost of registration | No government fee was found on the pages read; professional help costs money. | Unverified – check [gst.gov.in](https://www.gst.gov.in/) |

## Income tax

| Point | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Is the income taxable? | Yes, as income of the seller; report it in the income-tax return. Which ITR form and whether presumptive taxation applies were not researched. | Unverified detail – CA |
| TDS / TCS by marketplaces | Etsy withholds 0.1% TDS (5% without PAN/GSTIN) and 0.5% GST TCS; claim credit when filing. | Verified – [Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/11817502933783) |
| US tax forms | US platforms usually ask non-US sellers for a W-8BEN. Treaty rates and article to claim: not verified. | Unverified |

## Foreign exchange and bank
- Keep payout statements and the bank's inward remittance advice/FIRA for each transfer; they help with GST export proof and income records. (Practice, not a verified legal requirement.)
- Indian banks may deduct their own inward-remittance charges. Not quantified.

## What to tell the user, simply
1. "Etsy needs GST registration from you. Gumroad's pages don't say they need it."
2. "Below ₹20 lakh a year, the general rule doesn't force GST registration, but exports and online sales have special rules. Please ask a CA before you register or decide not to."
3. "Whatever you earn is taxable income. Keep a record of every payout."
