# Marketplace facts for an India-based seller (US buyers, digital downloads)

**Last checked: 2026-10-30.** Policies and fees change often. Treat every row as a starting point and re-check the linked official page before telling the user to sign up, and always before quoting a fee as current. If a page cannot be opened, say so and keep the status as "re-check".

Status labels used below:
- **Verified** – read on the platform's or government's own page on the date above.
- **Partly verified** – the main fact is on an official page, but a detail (amount, India-specific rule) was not stated there.
- **Unverified** – only third-party or community sources; do not present as fact.

## Etsy

| Item | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Can new Indian sellers open a shop? | Yes. "Sellers in India can now open new shops on Etsy." New shops opened in India "can only sell internationally" (fine for US buyers). | Verified – [Etsy Help: accept payments as a seller in India](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/6742925359255) |
| Upfront cost | One-time **US $10** set-up fee during onboarding for India. This breaks a strict ₹0 budget. | Verified – same page |
| GST | Must verify a **valid, regular GSTIN** after sign-up. Without it Etsy "will suspend your shop and withhold any deposits". This is an Etsy rule, separate from whether GST law would require registration. | Verified – same page |
| PAN | Personal PAN required (for business sellers, the primary owner's personal PAN). | Verified – same page |
| Payout method | Payoneer account required. Etsy deposits **USD only** to Payoneer every Monday; Payoneer then auto-withdraws to the Indian bank. | Verified – same page |
| Payment processing (India) | **5% + ₹25** per order. | Verified – same page |
| Listing fee | **US $0.20** per listing; lasts 4 months or until sold (auto-renew re-charges). | Verified – [Etsy Sell page](https://www.etsy.com/In-En/Sell) |
| Transaction fee | **6.5%** of sale price. | Verified – Etsy Sell page |
| Currency conversion | **2.5%** when listing currency differs from payment-account currency. List in USD to avoid it (deposits are USD). | Verified – Etsy Sell page |
| Offsite Ads | **15%** on sales from Offsite Ads; optional unless 12-month sales cross Etsy's threshold. | Verified – Etsy Sell page |
| Indian tax withheld by Etsy | **0.5% GST TCS** and **0.1% income-tax TDS** (TDS can be **5%** if PAN/GSTIN missing or non-compliant). | Verified – [Etsy Help: what Etsy withholds for India](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/11817502933783) |
| Full fee policy page | etsy.com/legal/fees blocked automated reading on the check date. | Re-check manually |
