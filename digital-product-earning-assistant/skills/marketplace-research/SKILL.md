# Marketplace research and eligibility

Goal: recommend one marketplace the user can actually use from India, with honest costs, before they spend time listing.

## Ground rules
- Start from `references/marketplace-facts.md` and `references/india-tax-compliance.md`. They hold dated, sourced findings.
- If browsing is available, re-check every fact you rely on against the official page linked there. If the reference is older than 60 days, or the user is about to sign up, re-check first. Update the date you checked in your answer.
- Use only official platform help/pricing pages and Indian government sources (gst.gov.in, cbic-gst.gov.in, incometax.gov.in) as proof. Blogs and forums can point you to a question but never settle it.
- Label every fact **Verified (source, date)**, **Partly verified**, or **Unverified**. Never fill a gap with a guess. Say "I could not confirm this" instead.
- "No monthly fee" is not "free". Count set-up fees, per-listing fees, percentage fees, fixed fees, processing, currency conversion, payout/withdrawal fees, annual account fees and minimum payout thresholds.
- Do not recommend Etsy without stating: US $10 set-up fee, mandatory GSTIN, Payoneer required.
- GST and income tax: give general information with sources, then recommend confirming with a CA. Never say "you don't need to register" as a definite answer.

## Eligibility checklist (run for each candidate)
1. Can an Indian resident open a seller account now?
2. GST: does the platform require a GSTIN? What does the law require at the user's turnover?
3. How buyers pay (cards, PayPal, UPI).
4. How the seller is paid (bank in INR, Payoneer, PayPal), payout schedule and **minimum payout**.
5. Listing, transaction, processing and marketplace fees.
6. Currency conversion and withdrawal charges.
7. Tax/compliance items: TDS/TCS withheld, US tax forms, sales tax/VAT handling, records to keep.

## Comparison workflow
1. **Ask one question if unknown:** "Is ₹0 strict, or could you pay a small one-time fee (about US $10–30) later?"
2. **Shortlist 2–3 platforms** that pass step 1 of the checklist. Default shortlist today: Gumroad (no upfront cost, INR bank payouts), Etsy (big search traffic, but $10 + GSTIN + Payoneer), TPT (best teacher audience, but $29 fee).
3. **Fill the comparison table** (template below), with sources and status per cell.
4. **Work one example** with the user's planned price, e.g. a $4 sale, showing gross → each fee → estimated net in USD and approximate INR. Mark conversion and payout costs as ranges where only ranges are published.
5. **Recommend one platform** and give one reason, one risk, and one next step.

Typical outcome under a strict ₹0 budget: start on **Gumroad** (no upfront fee, INR bank payout), knowing the **$100 minimum payout** means the first sales stay in the balance for a while. Move to Etsy or TPT only after the first sale proves demand and the user accepts the set-up fee and GST work. Re-check this conclusion whenever the facts change.

## Worked fee example (from the 2026-10-30 facts, $4.00 sale, one item)
- **Gumroad, direct link:** 10% + $0.50 = $0.90; card processing 2.9% + $0.30 = $0.42 → about **$2.68** before INR conversion by the payout provider (rate not published).
- **Etsy:** listing $0.20 + transaction 6.5% ($0.26) + processing 5% ($0.20) + ₹25 (≈ $0.30, depends on exchange rate) → about **$3.04** in USD; then Payoneer 1–4% conversion, 0.6% TDS + TCS withheld, plus the $10 set-up fee and possible $29.95 Payoneer annual fee.
- **TPT Basic:** 55% of $4 = $2.20 − $0.30 = **$1.90**, after the one-time $29 fee; Hyperwallet transfer fees vary.
